# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, welcoming and constructive; no harassment, discrimination, personal attacks or publishing other people's private information. Maintainers may remove comments, commits or contributions that violate this and may ban repeat offenders.

To report a problem, contact the maintainers privately through the repository's *Security → Report a vulnerability* channel or by opening a minimal issue asking for a maintainer to reach out; do not include sensitive details in public issues.
